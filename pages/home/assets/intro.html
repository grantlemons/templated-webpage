I’m Grant, a passionate software engineer and a proponent of free software and copyleft licensing.

I love the way that communities of passionate people form just to make something cool. I also generally think that, while the internet is a really cool way for people to meet and organize, technology companies have not been forces for good, and tend to twist the web in any way they can to abuse people's trust and desire for community for extractive purposes. For this reason, recently I've been feeling less confident in my belief in open licensing, especially now that LLM training has ramped up this extraction to a huge degree.

Improvement is important to me, so I am constantly challenging myself by using tools with steep learning curves that emphasize power over accessibility or ease of use. Don't confuse this for the whole productivity culture thing that prioritizes efficiency over all else. The most important thing for me is the process. While I like accomplishing things, what I enjoy most is spending a whole day in a flow state working on software. For that reason I'm not a big fan of using development tools that remove that part of my work (cough cough LLMs).
